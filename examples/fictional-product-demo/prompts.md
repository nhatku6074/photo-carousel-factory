# Demo prompts

All five images use the `photorealistic-natural` use case and are generated without captions. The fictional product is a matte sage-green reusable bottle with a tan loop lid and a small embossed mountain symbol; it has no wordmark.

## Slide 1 — Hook

A four-panel vertical collage showing desk clutter, phone distraction, task switching, and a clean reset moment. Use a dark center band for the later headline.

## Slide 2 — Clutter

A fictional remote worker starting the day at an overcrowded desk. Leave a quiet upper-middle area for the caption.

## Slide 3 — Phone

A fictional worker reaching toward a glowing phone beside a laptop. Leave a clean caption-safe wall area.

## Slide 4 — No reset

A fictional worker switching between several tasks without pausing, surrounded by open tabs, notes, and two workstations. Preserve a low-detail caption zone.

## Slide 5 — Product reveal

A fictional worker in a calm morning workspace holding the matte sage-green bottle with tan loop lid and mountain symbol. Keep the bottle sharp, upright, realistically scaled, and free of text.
